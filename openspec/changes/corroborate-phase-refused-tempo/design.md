## Admission and selection

Run only after the existing neural/key joins, staged input verification and neural
estimate, before method serialization. The neural result must be unavailable v5
with evaluated outcome insufficient, no real resource/search refusal, no global
ambiguity or local conflict, eligible normal long-source support and two genuine
probes. Reuse existing numeric eligibility/outcome rules. Recomputed fractional
max residual and RMS penalty must pass their unchanged limits; only its phase
difference may fail the existing5ms condition. Neither an integer pass nor stored
close-to-expected metrics may bypass this predicate.

Run unchanged QM once on the same protected complete playback PCM, then the
existing scalar kernel. QM must independently return an admitted value through
its own unchanged residual, rival, probe and integer/fractional checks. Require
finite same-unit period agreement:

`abs(QM_selected_period-NN_free_period)*(min(source_extent,QM_analyzer_extent)/NN_free_period)<=0.005`

NN_free_period is the original winner's free period; QM_selected_period is
60 divided by its independently admitted scalar. No octave folding, phase
alignment or convenient cropped reference. Use the admitted QM scalar exactly;
never relabel the failed NN check as passing or force integer rounding.
QM analyzer frames must equal exactly ceil(loaded_frames*44100/loaded_rate),
with origin0 and the recorded actual hop. Loaded rate/frame count must equal
the top-level NN playback domain. The min-extent formula cannot permit cropping.

## Evidence and limits

The frozen fifteen-source diagnostic reuses actual cold decode/playback and
normal mono conversion. Thirteen pairs have exact retained NN decoder/playback
identity; two historical controls lack those receipt fields and are not claimed
as paired. QM's default120BPM Rayleigh prior is explicit. Its errors on otherwise
good tracks disallow a blind override or QM-v4-over-NN-v5 confidence ordering.
Successful NN v4/v5 values, including known octave mismatches, stay unchanged
under this narrow feature. Nominal metrical selection remains a separate open
quality problem; coherent timing alone does not establish a producer's unit.
