# G3a accepted timing and control-only adoption

## Construction and identity

The accepted record owns the immutable G2 bound backend evidence. Raw construction
recomputes the complete supplied count-hypothesis summary. Comparable-attack
construction checks the exact verified PCM binding and recomputes the existing
full-source refinement with independent quarter evidence before fitting. Public,
mutable diagnostic reports cannot be promoted directly into accepted timing.
Only a uniquely supported verified candidate can construct a record; an explicit
policy/provenance assertion supplies acceptance, never fit quality alone.

The accepted period remains binary64, taken directly from the selected fit.
The independently selected signed origin has its own provenance and is separate
from source zero and the diagnostic intercept. The versioned SHA-256 revision
length-delimits strings, options and sequences and retains exact floating bits.
It binds source/PCM content and timebase, job/generation/request, raw/backend
configuration, timing-bound provenance, all evaluated counts and diagnostics,
fit/feature policies and evidence, accepted period/error state, origin and the
explicit acceptance assertion. A raw revision is not an accepted revision.

## Adoption state

A pure non-realtime guard retains verified current binding, timing intent,
accepted record and monotonically advancing revision. Each issued ticket belongs
to one guard and one current revision. New requests, source replacement, unload,
intent changes and successful adoption invalidate older tickets. Checked counter
exhaustion rejects rather than wrapping. Manual, TAP and legacy intent cannot
issue or adopt an automatic result. Failed adoption preserves current state.

This guard is not connected to the live engine in G3a. The future control owner
must feed actual current source/request metadata and every timing-intent change
under its publication ownership boundary. A caller-supplied matching snapshot
alone is not a live validity guarantee.

## Consumer audit and remaining G3 boundaries

Python editor/snap/automatic markers currently use binary64 scalar projection.
Native SourceGrid, transport master/reference BPM and BPMLOCK still derive from
binary32 parameters. MIDI signatures include effective BPM and endpoints, but
no accepted revision. Preparation identities and same-source stem checks use
their existing source/timebase rules. These consumers are deliberately unchanged
in G3a and must all use one accepted period/revision in the next bounded slice.

Musical duration versus independently rounded physical markers needs separate
75/1000-cycle, fractional-period/rate and callback-partition proof. Arithmetic
and source-coordinate checks cannot certify rendered DSP, onset or device SYNC.

## Validation

External public-API tests cover unchanged raw evidence, distinct half/double and
rational count identities, acceptance/feature/error/origin provenance, malformed
and unsupported inputs, binary64 fractional periods, source/request/intent races,
foreign tickets, repeated adoption and failure preserving accepted state. Full
project checks and official strict OpenSpec validation apply to this Rust slice.
