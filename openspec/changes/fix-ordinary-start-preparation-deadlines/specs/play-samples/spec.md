## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Ordinary preparation delay retains the original attack
An ordinary attack whose immutable preparation finishes after a planned execution endpoint SHALL retain its original content lifetime, source request, loop, volume, exclusive intent, sequence, input timestamp and target T. An expired endpoint SHALL NOT be adopted at another output frame by changing only scheduler or request labels. Before an actual effect at a new own E, the existing worker SHALL prepare that original's actual Native/FIFO at the complete selected physical source beginning under preserve-full-start-onset, without consuming unheard original-T-to-E output. Source/control and scalar gain/EQ stability guards SHALL remain. The callback SHALL use the sole existing scheduler and perform only bounded scalar checks and owner exchanges. The number of retained attacks, work attempts and elapsed frames SHALL remain bounded. Stop, cancellation, source/lifetime replacement, incompatible source or control changes, unavailable required material and reached resource/work bounds SHALL refuse without changing prior audio or inventing source history. Independent accepted ordinary attacks SHALL remain distinct and SHALL NOT invalidate each other merely by starting a new voice under an unchanged control program.

#### Scenario: A second ordinary worker returns after its planned endpoint
- **GIVEN** two original same-content attacks with distinct sequences and targets, unchanged source/control intent and independently owned preparation
- **WHEN** A actually starts and B's real worker is delayed beyond B's first planned E
- **THEN** B SHALL retain original T and obtain its own valid off-realtime prepared endpoint or report a declared bounded refusal
- **AND** ordinary worker delay alone within the admitted bounds SHALL NOT be relabelled prepared_mismatch
- **AND** A SHALL continue until B's own effect, whose complete emitted Native/FIFO/EQ beginning SHALL match the independently rendered complete beginning under each launch policy without slicing either output

### Requirement: Independent late preparations pipeline without reordering effects
The existing shared runtime SHALL let ready younger independent constant births prepare their own endpoints while an older same-content birth remains pending. Their effects SHALL retain original target and sequence order without adding one preparation lead per preceding attack. Each original SHALL retain its own preparation authority and Native or dry request. Nonstarting SET, genuine rate-glide and source-feed replay SHALL retain their existing execution route rather than entering fresh constant-birth reprepare. A transient placement or window observation during nonmutating late planning SHALL retain the same original pending work without changing its endpoint, status or attempt count. Global groups SHALL finish all-member nonmutating planning before changing any member endpoint; partial planning SHALL NOT be retried as a complete unchanged group. Repreparation SHALL be limited to eight attempts and 262144 output frames from original T; actual source/control changes or reached bounds SHALL preserve refusal behavior and the prior sounding voice.

#### Scenario: Several original workers miss one endpoint
- **GIVEN** eight distinct equal-target same-content originals whose real workers finish after the planned endpoint
- **WHEN** the returned work is prepared again through the existing shared runtime
- **THEN** each original obtains its own endpoint without a cumulative preparation lead per attack
- **AND** their effects retain sequence order and the final ordinary birth's complete emitted Native/FIFO/EQ beginning matches an independent actual birth without pre-emission source progression

#### Scenario: Dry piano and scalar SET retain their separate contracts
- **GIVEN** an older independent DSP birth still owns pending preparation
- **WHEN** a unity piano retrigger or a stopped scalar SET is admitted
- **THEN** the retrigger retains its genuine dry request for any later own preparation
- **AND** the nonstarting SET keeps its original scalar effect without entering fresh-birth ordering

#### Scenario: Late original or group overlaps a transient publication
- **GIVEN** a genuine independent original or global group has returned its own prepared work after the planned endpoint
- **WHEN** a temporary unrelated publication makes late planning Pending
- **THEN** every retained original keeps its own endpoint, status, attempt count and prepared owner unchanged while waiting
- **AND** a group changes no member endpoint until all member plans are available
- **AND** stable planning later prepares the same originals at their own valid endpoint, with a common endpoint and atomic effect for a group

### Requirement: Immutable ordinary preparation does not require a physical slot publication
The ordinary worker SHALL validate immutable inputs through its captured stable content cell and exact original source, timing, window, launch and action guards. An unrelated in-progress scalar publication marker SHALL NOT itself revoke those inputs. This worker validation SHALL grant no physical slot or effect authority. Control publication and final callback adoption SHALL independently retain their coherent placement, own ACK and complete original effect claims.

#### Scenario: An unrelated slot briefly owns the global publication marker
- **GIVEN** an ordinary original owns valid immutable source/preparation inputs
- **WHEN** its actual worker overlaps a different slot's scalar publication without any source or content change
- **THEN** immutable preparation SHALL remain valid through that marker
- **AND** the original SHALL still require its own actual callback placement and complete effect claim before it can start audio

### Requirement: A proved original capture may wait for final placement observation
After an ordinary capture has successfully validated its actual source binding, generation, request incarnation, timing and launch authority, an exclusively Pending final placement observation SHALL retain that same opaque original owner for the existing asynchronous readiness path. It SHALL grant neither publication nor audio-effect authority while placement remains Pending. A missing initial source binding SHALL NOT be replaced by another occupant or newly captured source as a consequence of this rule.

#### Scenario: A scalar publication begins after the original source was captured
- **GIVEN** one actual original source and its work have passed complete capture admission
- **WHEN** an unrelated scalar publication makes only the final placement observation Pending
- **THEN** the capture SHALL remain the same waiting owner with its original timestamp, content key and sequence
- **AND** a later stable observation SHALL still require the original complete publication and callback claims
- **AND** an actual Stop or source retirement before publication SHALL prevent that owner from starting audio
