## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Existing window profiles are compared through actual original-owned output
The bounded test evaluation MUST compare explicit Short/Together and Medium/Together
Native options through the existing original source/ACK, accepted private map delivery,
captured owning preparation and mapped renderer. An independent direct C-ABI oracle
SHALL use separate handles, explicit matching options and independent source equations
and profile-window history bounds. Actual complete PCM, coordinate, protocol, partition
or custody mismatches MUST fail the test. Existing production constructors, guards,
clock, renderer and realtime paths MUST remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: Medium classification readahead changes the observed response
- **WHEN** the predeclared profile matrix is rendered under genuine existing ownership
- **THEN** its complete output is compared with its independently prepared C-ABI stream
- **AND** Short's earlier results and all unchanged acoustic acceptance criteria remain visible

### Requirement: Nominal alignment preserves original response and separates emission policy
Each comparison MUST declare Cnom from actual post-preparation pre-signal Native delay
plus the511-frame adapter lead before observing response output. Complete raw and
natural output SHALL remain available, including negative-time samples on the nominal
timeline. No fitted offset, gain, crop, replacement reference or extended wrapped
capture SHALL manufacture a pass. Timing, peak/energy retention and complete-response
criteria MUST remain unchanged. FirstSound gate retention and necessary earlier-emission
constraints SHALL be reported separately and SHALL NOT grant actual S/E scheduling,
intended-output qualification or productive admission.

#### Scenario: Nominal timing improves but gating loses the original first attack
- **WHEN** a nominally aligned response meets timing but its proposed gate loses required energy or peak
- **THEN** the retention failure remains explicit against the complete original raw response
- **AND** no FirstSound, AttackAtGrid, public SYNC or Human acceptance is inferred

### Requirement: Supplemental tone windows preserve evidence eligibility and original negatives
The comparison MUST retain original8192-frame tone results and separately report
nominal windows and predeclared24576-frame windows within the first two constant
canonical segments. Full-window, segment and capture bounds SHALL be checked without
truncation. Existing crossing-count, energy and pitch-error thresholds MUST remain.
Each family SHALL retain separate denominators, failures and unavailable states.

#### Scenario: A low tone lacks enough cycles in the original window
- **WHEN** a longer predeclared eligible window supplies additional evidence
- **THEN** that result is reported separately from the original window's ineligibility
- **AND** no earlier negative measurement or acoustic gate is removed
