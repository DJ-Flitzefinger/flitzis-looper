## Why

Slow timing changes can leave a usable song without automatic BPM when precise
constant-tempo checks refuse it. The performer requests a final interior mean,
visible uncertainty and protection against downgrading a supported precise result.

## What Changes

Add a source-bound final QM/mean fallback and schema10 evidence. Keep successful
precise paths first. Use three fixed interior sections and both detectors for a
mean; display English possible-drift labels. Preserve source/request ownership,
manual intent, prior precise same-source results and the ordinary scalar runtime.

## Impact

Native offline analysis and budget/rollback, strict Python provenance/Copy/restore,
loader merge and PAD/BPM presentation. No new realtime tempo-following mechanism.
