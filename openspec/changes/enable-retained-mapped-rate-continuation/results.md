# Retained mapped Native rate continuation — software evidence

An already playing ordinary mapped Native voice can now follow a master-tempo
change when its current logical and actual-input endpoints coincide at the real
execution frame. The existing global coordinator claims the entire cohort before
any effect. Native identity, FIFO contents, partial block-head pitch, original
source admission and historical raw provenance remain owned by that stream.

This is one checked implementation step inside the unchanged full pre-Rust Goal.
It does not complete the connected milestone or replace Human/device acceptance.

## Implementation and measured cost

The existing boxed source owner separates immutable birth projection from its
claimed current origin. Two numeric result slots per role reuse only successful
results of the unchanged strict factory. Every source, ACK, lifetime, Native,
continuation-stamp and canonical-checkpoint guard still executes. A real cut clears
Current keys; Birth results never become current authority.

Mapped/phase reader requests up to the existing 1024-frame processing bound prepare
exact interpolation taps once, validate every channel and current/outgoing reader
for the entire chunk, then write with the original interpolation and transition
gains. Scalar, zero-length, longer and ineligible invalid-domain requests retain
the original paths and error ordering. There is no new scheduler or reader owner.

The original uninstrumented Release test was retained byte-for-byte (1419 bytes,
SHA256 `9e16b0ffab4776677f31f81743ad5b8da5ef31081710ff17764f46fa07d3a765`).
Its actual claim plus 512-frame render at 48 kHz produced:

| Actual independent PADs | Before, maximum ms | After, maximum ms |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 2 | 20.7061 | 1.9506 |
| 4 | 43.0850 | 3.9127 |
| 6 | 76.0252 | 5.9417 |
| 10, synthetic stress | 124.6985 | 9.8859 |

These are one warm-up and three measured repetitions of the declared fixture,
with no Rust allocation requests. The nominal 512-frame duration is 10.6667 ms.
The separate paired diagnostic also preserves exact PCM and complete Native/FIFO
state; its ten-PAD samples include 11.9208 ms combined and 11.4227 ms installed
render outliers. Those results remain negative stress evidence. They do not
establish a device guarantee for seven through ten PADs. Productive optimization
targets two, four and six PADs; problems/glitches above ten remain possible, and
the application has no new ten-PAD cap.

## Remaining connected requirements

wet phase/Hold paths, finite original EOF and genuinely qualified early Attack
remain open. Mode/tempo/map ACK is not proof of grid phase. BeatThis reference/B2,
default-cutover, paired-session and real Human music/device acceptance gates also
remain open; RAW/synthetic/unit evidence cannot substitute for them. Existing
implementation and this measured improvement must be reused when completing those
requirements. No full-Goal completion or Rust-port/Slice8 authorization is implied.
