## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Superseded restored verification settles its exact owners durably
Verified saved stem settlement SHALL distinguish confirmed Python source, content or cache replacement from temporary Native admission failure. A confirmed obsolete settlement SHALL use the existing superseded-selection retirement path and SHALL retain its exact lease, prepared pair and retirement reservation until the current replacement configuration is durable. It SHALL NOT register the obsolete pair against replacement content, discard shared selected files directly or overwrite newer PAD feedback. A still-current Native registration failure SHALL retain the original preparation for its established retry path. No settlement work SHALL enter the realtime callback.

#### Scenario: Source, content or saved cache changes during restored settlement
- **GIVEN** a verified restored pair retained owners after an assignment or registration refusal
- **WHEN** its original source, content or cache is replaced or unloaded
- **THEN** current configuration is flushed before exact old ownership is retired
- **AND** replacement state and newer feedback remain intact without registration replay

#### Scenario: Replacement persistence fails temporarily
- **GIVEN** the old restored settlement is superseded and replacement configuration is unwritten
- **WHEN** persistence fails
- **THEN** its exact owners and reservation remain retained for a later settlement attempt
- **AND** retirement succeeds only after the durable file contains the current replacement

#### Scenario: Current registration remains under temporary pressure
- **GIVEN** the verified pair still matches the exact Python source, content and cache
- **WHEN** Native registration is temporarily refused
- **THEN** the original preparation and retry remain without being classified as superseded
