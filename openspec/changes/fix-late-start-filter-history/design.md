# Continuation and fresh physical birth

The 2026-10-20 full-onset contract separates genuine existing-source continuation
from ordinary and fresh Piano SET_RETRIGGER births. SET without Retrigger, resume
and actual source-feed replay retain their history/lifetime semantics. A fresh
birth retains its complete selected beginning and starts a new filter chronology
with actual emission at E; no E-T interval is consumed as unheard new content.
Original T and original scalar source/DSP/gain/ABA guards remain mandatory.

For a supported glide, the actual accepted rate, rate target and remaining
active-frame smoothing interval are evaluated at E independently of resetting
physical source phase. The source reset must not reset that clock or substitute
its final target. The same existing source reader, Native candidate, worker and
atomic claim prepare and adopt the new beginning. Unproved past-T mappings,
paused pending glides and unsupported transition/read histories remain refused.
Neither this distinction nor a full beginning establishes acoustic alignment.

## Former-contract output evidence

The following source/profile-specific suffix recipe and successful comparisons
are retained as former-contract evidence and for genuinely compatible existing-source
continuation. They are not the current fresh-birth output contract.

The on-time reference owns an independent source, Native processor and PAD filter. It starts at original T and renders through the interval that is inaudible in the delayed fixture. Every sample emitted by the delayed fixture must participate in the suffix comparison.

Filter reconstruction uses the existing ordinary preparation lane and exact output chronology. A reset-at-T fixed DSP chain processes only the audible output interval from T through E, including its actual upstream gain contract. Native pre-T preparation is excluded from filter history. Dry starts require their existing source output without unnecessary Native processing. Candidate readiness and the original commit recheck source, DSP/gain intent and chronological authority, including ABA revocation. Unproved past trajectories are rejected before live state changes; no later controls are invented as past history.

Transfer fixed prepared filter state only under the same successful original placement/effect claim as the corresponding audio. Preserve ordinary on-time behavior, first-live feedback, other PADs, bounded callback work and off-thread preparation/retirement.

Small dry histories keep the already available callback E. Work construction allocates up to eight 32-frame planar scratch buffers on the control side. The callback clones only the existing fixed filter chain, resets the clone, and reuses the existing checked fractional reader. One control-drain budget limits all actions and groups to 128 channel-frames, with a 32-frame per-action limit. No Native, allocation or coefficient construction occurs in this bounded operation. Larger histories use the same worker with a DSP-only lead candidate of 512 frames. This is an implementation candidate requiring callback/host readiness measurement, not a claimed 48 kHz deadline guarantee; backlog and original readiness guards remain authoritative.

The former moving-Piano recipe captured a reset-at-T checkpoint and the history's
unshifted T endpoint, then advanced the same action-owned Native and fresh filter
through T to E. Its independent Raw Native oracle certified that T-born suffix
contract. This result remains useful chronology/ownership evidence but cannot
certify the complete beginning required of a fresh physical retrigger. Current
fresh-birth references must declare physical origin and accepted rate state at E
independently, include every first emitted sample, and apply no alignment crop.
