The selected fractional BPM can already have independent regional support while
its additional integer/fractional counterprobe remains unmeasured. The retained
FLAC example has two compatible strict thirds, but no eligible historical middle,
whole-source or maximal-run common region under policy four.

Policy five adds one narrowly defined fallback: fit the complete continuous hull
of all published compatible canonical thirds, retaining every intervening original
event. Both exact candidate periods use that one mask and pivot. The selected
fractional BPM remains unchanged. The supported source interval is explicit and
does not imply whole-song, musical or scientific timing acceptance.

Existing versions one through four remain readable with their original rules.
This change does not refresh a valid old cache automatically, change raw evidence,
alter loop/grid/manual/TAP intent, or modify SYNC.
