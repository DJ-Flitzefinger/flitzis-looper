## Why
Verified saved stem pairs can retain their preparation, lease and retirement
reservation indefinitely if their PAD source or cache is replaced during a
retryable assignment or registration failure.

## What Changes
Settle confirmed obsolete restored verification through the existing durable
superseded-selection retirement path, preserving current-selection retries and
replacement feedback.

## Non-goals
No new audio path, source recapture, stem regeneration or callback work.

## Impact
Python offline settlement and existing persistence/asset ownership only. Native
timing, FIFO, live readers and realtime admission remain authoritative.
