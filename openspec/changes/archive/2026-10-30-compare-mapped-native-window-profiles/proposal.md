## Why

The original owning-Native measurements preserved exact C-ABI PCM but failed the
unchanged acoustic criteria. Their extreme-pitch transient deformation cannot be
fixed by a constant offset. A concrete existing backend variant with classification
readahead remains unevaluated through the same actual source and Native owners.

## What Changes

- Compare existing Short/Together and Medium/Together Native options on the same
  complete original-qualified mapped producer and independent direct C-ABI oracle.
- Keep natural output results and separately evaluate a nominal timeline declared
  from the handle's pre-signal delay and adapter lead, without fitting or discarding data.
- Measure full-response retention separately from timing and report the necessary
  FirstSound/early-emission constraints without granting actual scheduling authority.
- Supplement tone evidence with fixed longer windows while preserving every original result.

## Non-goals

No productive backend switch, new DSP renderer, nominal compensation, guard opening,
new scheduler/clock, automatic musical map qualification, B2 default cutover, actual
early emission or Human/device/hearing acceptance. Earlier negative results remain.

## Realtime

All additions are bounded test-only construction, capture and analysis. Production
callback code, allocation/locking policy, original retirement and constructors stay unchanged.
