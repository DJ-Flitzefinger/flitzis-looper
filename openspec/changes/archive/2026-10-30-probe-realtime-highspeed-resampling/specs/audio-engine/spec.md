## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A distinct realtime resampling-order prerequisite preserves source and criteria
The bounded test-only HighSpeed experiment SHALL use the predeclared canonical
q1/p2 input, actual pre-signal P/N and its own unity control. It MUST retain full
Raw output, original energy/peak and fixed complete nominal end windows under
both callers. Existing timing/energy/tail criteria and HC evidence MUST remain
unchanged. No fitted compensation, fabricated output or productive admission is
authorized by the experiment.

#### Scenario: The new signal order fails one prerequisite
- **WHEN** any of the four kick cases or own unity controls fails its acoustic criteria
- **THEN** the candidate is reported rejected with actual positive and negative measurements
- **AND** no broad matrix, guard opening or full SYNC acceptance is claimed

#### Scenario: A shared capture helper is refactored
- **WHEN** the new profile shares the existing realtime owner and scorers
- **THEN** old HC full Raw hashes, canonical inputs, coordinates and metrics reproduce exactly
- **AND** new HS results remain separately identified and independently scored
