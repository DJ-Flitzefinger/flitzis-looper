## Bounded original ownership

The existing CapturedPadStart remains the original authority for content lifetime,
source request, loop, volume, exclusive flag, sequence, input timestamp and target
T. A preparation attempt's planned E is not permission to adopt its Native/FIFO
at a later frame. Late work must remain owned by that same bounded action cell
until it either obtains actual off-realtime preparation at its new own endpoint
or honestly reaches a declared bound/refusal. Original T is never recalculated.

The implementation must preserve the actual original-T Native birth and every
T-to-E output consumed by its independent filter state. The existing retained
Normal-start preparation and worker continuation are the available foundations;
an endpoint relabel, fresh mutable bank capture or callback catch-up is insufficient.
Source/control ABA, Stop, cancellation, source/lifetime replacement, unsupported
trajectory and actual resource exhaustion remain refusals with old audio intact.

Worker validity uses the retained stable content cell, own source/history pins,
action epoch and original scalar guards. A global publication marker alone says
nothing about those immutable inputs. Physical control placement and actual
callback publication/adoption keep their respective coherent placement checks.

## Deterministic evidence

A sequence-and-engine-scoped test-only worker gate pauses one actual ordinary
owner before preparation; other tests and other owners continue normally. The
two-birth test lets A actually start, holds B beyond B's planned E, and then
releases B. Both launch policies must preserve A through B's own effect. Every
emitted B sample, its Native/FIFO ownership, source cursor and nonneutral EQ/Trim
are compared with a separately prepared on-time original-T render.

A separate gate overlaps actual worker preparation with an unrelated slot's
existing publication marker. That marker performs no source/placement change.
Worker success does not grant slot authority: the same original must still pass
its actual final callback claim after the marker ends.

## Integrated endpoint preparation

The shared runtime retains the original action receipt while its worker owns the
heavy work. A returned independent constant birth can reprepare at a new endpoint
at most eight times, with at most 262144 output frames from original T. Each
attempt retains its genuine original-T checkpoint and its own Native handle,
and computes the complete own Native/FIFO/EQ chronology off realtime. Bounds,
unsupported source-feed replay and genuine control changes remain refusals.

Ready younger births prepare concurrently with an older pending birth. Their
planned effect cannot precede an older same-content independent birth; equal
effects preserve the existing original sequence order. Nonstarting SET retains
its original scheduling route, as do genuine rate-glide and source-feed replay
work. Only an eligible independent constant birth enters fresh reprepare. A unity
piano birth keeps its own dry request so
later DSP preparation still has genuine authority. Fixed scalar receipt metadata
is cleared while real work still holds the owners, before off-realtime retirement.

A transient placement/window observation during late planning retains the same
original work and leaves its endpoint, status and attempt count unchanged. Global
groups first compute every member's scalar endpoint/DSP plan in a fixed stack
array, without mutating any member. Pending in that phase retains the intact
group. Only a complete plan proceeds to the existing own-receipt commit and
common-endpoint worker publication. A definitive concurrent source/cancellation
or commit failure rejects and retires the entire group; a partially committed
group is never retried as though its original endpoints were unchanged.

Additional regressions cover both wet piano policies at 1.43 with Key Lock,
eight equal-target independent originals without one extra preparation lead per
attack, a queued dry piano birth and a nonstarting SET. The original source-control
ABA and maximum catch-up cancellation cases preserve the old sounding voice.

A final placement observation may wait only after a complete original capture.
An already unavailable initial coherent binding remains an admission refusal;
this change never creates an original owner by retrying a mutable PAD identifier.
