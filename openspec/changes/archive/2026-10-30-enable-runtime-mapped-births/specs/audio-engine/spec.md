## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Public fresh mapped births share original runtime map authority
Ordinary, global and fresh Piano SetRetrigger births SHALL reuse one admitted
original map capsule and Ready runtime geometry, including supported General and
FullFraction classes. Source/content/OwnACK/cancellation and exact canonical
playback provenance MUST survive preparation, final claim and retirement.

#### Scenario: A genuine decimal map starts through GUI, MIDI or fresh piano
- **WHEN** its original selected map, physical source and current read projection qualify
- **THEN** the existing birth pipeline reads the original physical beginning at actual E
- **AND** no legacy-only reconstruction or copied equal-domain geometry grants authority

### Requirement: Fresh pitch retriggers reuse explicit absent-map launch permission
Fresh SetRetrigger SHALL retain requested Mapped policy and MAY derive actual
Legacy only from the same explicit current ordinary_pending source provenance
used by ordinary launch. This permission MUST NOT apply to live Set/edit/mode/seek
or a withheld, invalid, stale or unqualified selected map.

#### Scenario: SYNC is requested before an effective map exists
- **WHEN** a fresh retrigger consumes the single admitted launch region/selection row
- **THEN** complete ordinary playback remains available with requested SYNC pending
- **AND** a second lookup or mapped=None alone cannot supply fallback permission

### Requirement: Actual-E birth preserves original T and the complete source beginning
A fresh mapped birth SHALL retain immutable T/M(T)/LaunchQualification separately
from its actual-E anchored read clock. Moved E or read projection MUST cause full
fresh-A candidate preparation with existing retirement and final atomic checks.
No E-T source skip, M(E)-as-M(T), old-FIFO retag or fabricated paused proof is allowed.

#### Scenario: Preparation misses T or the master changes before effect
- **WHEN** the current E/read projection differs from the prepared candidate
- **THEN** the candidate is replaced or honestly remains pending/refused
- **AND** the eventual effect preserves original T and the entire physical beginning

### Requirement: Mapped Key Lock owns Native from birth and pending Attack stays truthful
Both classification and capture SHALL reserve Native for qualified mapped Key
Lock even at initial unity. Physical birth SHALL preserve H silence/D=H-(B-1)/C=0,
including source quiet prefixes. FirstSound SHALL enforce no-earlier-than-T without
trim-to-first-nonzero. Requested AttackAtGrid without a qualified anchor SHALL
remain requested and may play complete output with pending/no_qualified_anchor;
it MUST NOT claim effective Attack, FirstSound substitution or confirmed phase.

#### Scenario: A map begins at unity and later changes rate
- **WHEN** its fresh ordinary or piano birth prepares and starts
- **THEN** the same original-qualified Native/FIFO owner continues through the rate changes
- **AND** both requested policies preserve complete initial PCM and truthful alignment state

#### Scenario: A new bank source is published without replacing the running voice
- **WHEN** the old mapped Native voice retains its own admitted source and lifetime
- **THEN** its complete PCM and FIFO continue after publication and a refused new-source request
- **AND** the new source cannot borrow the old birth, readiness or emission observation
- **AND** explicit assignment replacement still stops the old voice after its successful claim

### Requirement: Birth feedback proves only its source-bound first mixed interval
Optional birth feedback SHALL be published atomically with current content/source
ownership and conservative mixed-voice aggregation. FirstSound earliest_bound_met
SHALL require the birth's exact first complete qualified mixed interval at E>=T;
later suffix reads or mode/freshness markers MUST NOT substitute that proof.
Historical reference qualification and emitted-to-mix MUST remain distinct from
current grid phase, hearing, musical and Human acceptance.

#### Scenario: The first read fails but a later suffix succeeds
- **WHEN** feedback is refreshed for that birth
- **THEN** it cannot gain onset/emission confirmation from the suffix
- **AND** replacement, malformed or partly unknown feedback cannot retain a prior positive

#### Scenario: An older Native reports no optional birth tuple
- **WHEN** current Python reads that otherwise valid source-bound feedback
- **THEN** birth is unknown and existing ordinary playback remains compatible

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Preserve the mapped Native output-alignment activation gate
Full effective grid-SYNC/phase approval SHALL remain unavailable until prepared
Native output is qualified for the intended output-time range and all retained
alignment gates. NativeInput coordinates, silent warm-up, Raw-Native agreement or
mode/rate application alone MUST NOT authorize that approval. This later candidate
policy explicitly permits original-qualified public mapped playback with complete
physical beginnings and unconfirmed alignment; it replaces the earlier blanket
public-start refusal only for that pending outcome, not the output-phase gate.
The original qualified dry envelope and all source/ACK/reader/retirement guards
SHALL remain intact. Pending playback MUST NOT be presented as complete SYNC or
effective AttackAtGrid.

#### Scenario: A qualified mapped Native candidate can play but has no output anchor
- **WHEN** the complete original beginning and exact actual-E ownership qualify
- **THEN** mode/rate playback may proceed with explicit unconfirmed alignment
- **AND** musical/output-phase approval and the complete milestone remain open
