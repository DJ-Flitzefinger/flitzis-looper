## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Own canonical mapped future projection
Internal mapped future preparation SHALL retain the original qualified immutable map and frozen epoch alongside current source/content/window/own-ACK authority. A capability SHALL originate from the actual captured owner and SHALL validate the checkpoint against that original canonical trajectory before projection. Equal-domain geometry or copied coordinates alone SHALL NOT grant permission. Projection SHALL update the playback and source plan atomically and SHALL refuse overflow, mismatched epochs and active transitions. Generic copied positive mapped projection SHALL remain refused.

#### Scenario: Future crosses many knots and wraps
- **WHEN** original-qualified internal future input crosses canonical knot or wrap boundaries
- **THEN** each read and exclusive endpoint SHALL follow the original trajectory at its actual intended address
- **AND** caller partitions SHALL NOT alter the trajectory, source selection or Native owner

#### Scenario: Copied checkpoint impersonates original geometry
- **WHEN** a checkpoint or epoch disagrees with the original qualified trajectory despite sharing its loop domain
- **THEN** projection SHALL refuse without partially changing the checkpoint or granting Native preparation authority

### Requirement: Preserve declared raw output and actual input chronology
Internal fixed-h Key Lock off physical-birth preparation MAY retain a raw suffix declared before observing output. For block B, silent prefix H and declared raw cut D it SHALL consume exactly D+B-1 adapter frames, of which exactly H are silence without source progress. Positive remaining input SHALL use the original canonical map and existing source readers. The existing preparation bound SHALL remain enforced. The retained ledger SHALL distinguish logical output T+j, raw output D+j and actual input T+(D+B-1-H)+j after j successful emitted frames. It SHALL retain the same Native and FIFO owners and SHALL NOT infer acoustic alignment from those labels.

#### Scenario: Actual input leads logical output across different knots
- **WHEN** the actual future cursor and logical cursor reach different canonical boundaries
- **THEN** each live chunk SHALL be bounded by both exclusive endpoints and both cursors SHALL refresh from the original owner
- **AND** only a successful source read and Native render SHALL advance provenance
- **AND** genuine NativeInput receipts SHALL name actual input rather than buffered output or callback-now

#### Scenario: Required future input is unavailable
- **WHEN** a pending candidate's source/content/window/own-ACK or original action authority is stale or cancelled, coverage is insufficient, or retained provenance is invalid
- **THEN** preparation or processing SHALL refuse without substituting scalar input, partially advancing the ledger or releasing a required reader
- **AND** original map/source owners SHALL remain safe through existing off-realtime retirement
- **AND** a superseded selected-map request alone SHALL NOT invalidate an already effective voice's immutable original map ownership

### Requirement: Keep public mapped output admission guarded
Internal owning future and raw-suffix agreement SHALL NOT enable public nonzero mapped starts, mapped delayed execution, effective SYNC or either unproven emission policy. Public admission SHALL continue to require the canonical intended output range shared with dry participants and full beginning retention. Key Lock on, general mapped SourceHistory replay, catchup and active transitions SHALL remain unavailable in this internal path.

#### Scenario: Raw suffix agrees but intended output alignment is unproven
- **WHEN** internal output matches an independent raw Native suffix but public output alignment is unproven
- **THEN** public nonzero mapped admission SHALL remain refused with no effective mutation
- **AND** complete early output evidence and the original target SHALL be preserved rather than hidden by cropping, source skips or clock shifts
