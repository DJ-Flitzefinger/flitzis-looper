## Source program and independent births

The callback already keeps source/control revisions by content cell and a fixed
constant-interval observation. The observation currently lives on a voice and its
exact logical checkpoint intentionally fails across an arbitrary phase change.
A new admitted Normal launch changes that voice's phase and DSP ownership without
necessarily changing its immutable source/loop/rate program.

Only the complete existing launch claim may carry an already proved constant
observation across its actual effect E. The pre-birth observation must reach E
exactly and retain its original content/source/reader/domain/revision contract.
The previous active unpaused voice's admitted content key must match the original
action key, even when another content owns identical physical PCM. Missing voice
admission is an unavailable continuation, not a reason to infer one from the bank.
The new checkpoint must have the same constant rate and Normal domain. The carried
observation rebases its expected logical checkpoint to the independently prepared
new launch at E; it carries no Native/FIFO/PCM journal or old voice lifetime.
The existing at-most-one-active-voice-per-content admission remains required.

Stop, Pause, arbitrary Seek, source replacement, source/control ABA, reader/stem or
loop changes, nonconstant rate trajectories, missing reads and output-clock gaps
or reversal retain their refusal behavior. A later frozen piano pitch tuple is
not a change to the independent source-rate program. Gain/EQ history keeps its
existing independent DspInputIntent authority.

## Realtime ownership

The continuation is bounded scalar state in the existing callback path. Native
construction, complete-beginning preparation, source pins and retirement stay on their
existing non-realtime owners. No callback I/O, Python/GIL, logging, blocking lock,
new heap allocation, replay or unbounded search is introduced.
