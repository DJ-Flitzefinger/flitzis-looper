## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Preserve the mapped Native output-alignment activation gate
Public nonzero-pitch mapped starts SHALL remain unavailable until prepared Native output represents the same intended output-time range as the canonical trajectory and dry participants. Input coordinates, silent warm-up, constant h or a delayed Raw-Native match alone SHALL NOT authorize productive admission or effective SYNC. The original qualified dry envelope SHALL remain compatible.

#### Scenario: Future fixed-pitch request has no proven output alignment
- **WHEN** a qualified future mapped start requests nonzero pitch without the required output alignment
- **THEN** it SHALL report mapped timing unavailable before effective mutation
- **AND** it SHALL preserve preceding audio without scalar fallback, implicit Key Lock change or successful mapped-effective feedback

### Requirement: Prepare qualified fixed-pitch mapped input through existing Native ownership
Internal fixed-h preparation with Key Lock off SHALL retain original qualified owning geometry and current source/content/window/own-ACK authority. It SHALL reuse SourcePlayback, the source reader and NativeAdapterState. Default physical-birth warm-up SHALL consume only silence before the captured beginning without moving its canonical origin. Copied coordinates alone SHALL NOT grant map or source authority. This preparation SHALL NOT activate the public Native mapped path.

#### Scenario: Component reads cross fractional knots and wraps
- **GIVEN** original qualified geometry and current source authority retained through internal preparation
- **WHEN** real reads cross fractional knots or wraps
- **THEN** input coordinates and exclusive endpoints SHALL follow the canonical trajectory at actual intended addresses
- **AND** successful reads SHALL publish genuine NativeInput receipts
- **AND** constant h SHALL NOT add another tempo multiplication or per-knot reset

#### Scenario: Uncropped output preserves delay and caller independence
- **WHEN** the component renders equivalent intervals using different legal caller partitions
- **THEN** complete output from frame zero SHALL agree with an independent Raw-Native stream including genuine delay
- **AND** sufficient bounded observation SHALL retain the complete source prefix and verify meaningful wet output without cropping or weakening amplitude/error bounds
- **AND** history limits SHALL remain unchanged and callback-now SHALL NOT relabel buffered input
- **AND** this match SHALL NOT establish public output alignment, perceived onset or musical/Human acceptance

### Requirement: Refuse unintegrated mapped delayed reconstruction before scalar comparison
Mapped delayed reconstruction SHALL preserve genuine custody/source/continuity checks and then refuse as mapped timing unavailable before a scalar endpoint comparison. It SHALL NOT partially mutate a voice, move its frozen target, skip content or enable mapped source-history, catchup, nondefault cuts or retained offsets without their complete owning chronology.

#### Scenario: Dry mapped group misses its target under retirement pressure
- **WHEN** an admitted dry mapped group cannot claim its frozen target due to retirement capacity
- **THEN** no participant SHALL partially adopt
- **AND** unintegrated mapped reprepare SHALL retain the original target, report mapped timing unavailable and preserve preceding audio and required owners
