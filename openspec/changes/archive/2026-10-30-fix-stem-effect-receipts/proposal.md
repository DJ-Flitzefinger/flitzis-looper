## Why

Ordinary stem mode or mask controls can change callback selection while an older
committed operation continues reporting effective. Same-callback ABA can restore
identical fields without restoring that earlier operation's authority.

## What Changes

Withdraw the superseded effective receipt at the accepted ordered callback
mutation. Preserve historical residency, the selected pair and every actual
source/reader/Native owner; keep rejected commands from claiming a state change.

## Non-goals

No DSP, ramp, source reset, reprepare, new lock or audio path. The callback change
is fixed scalar receipt state only; heavy lifetime retirement remains off realtime.
