## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Fresh physical launches retain their complete selected beginning
Each new ordinary PAD launch and fresh Piano SET_RETRIGGER birth SHALL retain the complete selected physical source beginning in its first actual emitted PCM. Preparation, input delivery or residency delay SHALL NOT consume E minus T as unheard new-source or filter output. Requested target T SHALL remain immutable, actual effect E SHALL remain truthful, and the new source playhead SHALL progress from its actual emitted beginning. A late full launch SHALL NOT be described as having retained the phase of an on-time T launch. Onset compensation SHALL NOT alter the action's admitted source identity, selected loop markers, BPM, pitch tuple, source-rate intent or an established master transport. The pre-existing explicitly selected pending one-time bootstrap MAY complete from that source's canonical next-read beat at actual E; this SHALL NOT invent a T-born source phase, select another reference or rearm a completed bootstrap. This rule supersedes original-T suffix recipes for fresh births in prepare-key-lock-realtime-state, fix-past-piano-retrigger, fix-independent-retrigger-history, fix-late-start-filter-history and fix-ordinary-start-preparation-deadlines. Genuine existing-source SET without Retrigger, resume and source-feed replay SHALL retain their separate history, rate and lifetime contracts.

#### Scenario: Ready dry source is published after original target
- **GIVEN** a PAD owns an immutable source with one unique first attack at its selected loop beginning
- **AND** original target T is captured before the real publication arrives
- **WHEN** its original launch takes effect at E after T
- **THEN** the emitted dry PCM SHALL start at the selected physical source beginning under the admitted rate
- **AND** its first attack is retained without a fixed offset, marker change or gain boost
- **AND** feedback retains requested T and actual E

#### Scenario: On-time and delayed dry starts share physical content
- **GIVEN** independent launches use the same selected source and constant rate
- **WHEN** one starts on time and another has a delayed publication
- **THEN** their complete emitted dry PCM beginning matches the same independent physical-source reference without slicing either emitted output
- **AND** an existing peer voice and an established global transport are unaffected

#### Scenario: Same and different Piano notes arrive after their original target
- **GIVEN** an original GUI or MIDI SET_RETRIGGER owns its exact selected source, pitch tuple and original T under the supported admission guards
- **WHEN** a same-note or different-note retrigger takes effect at E after T on a stopped, active or supported stationary paused PAD
- **THEN** the entire emitted PCM beginning SHALL match an independent full-beginning reference under the same admitted rate state and fresh filter chronology without slicing either output
- **AND** the original T, truthful E, source/timing/ACK owners and separate Native/FIFO candidate SHALL remain bound to that one attack
- **AND** the old voice and other PADs SHALL remain unchanged before the complete effect claim

#### Scenario: A selected pending session bootstrap first becomes playable
- **GIVEN** the existing dedicated one-time bootstrap already selected this source and has not completed
- **WHEN** its full physical beginning becomes playable at actual E
- **THEN** the existing bootstrap MAY anchor the canonical source beat at E without advancing source phase by E minus T
- **AND** requested T remains unchanged, and later retriggers SHALL preserve that established master epoch without rearming bootstrap

### Requirement: Fresh Native births declare physically silent pre-origin context
Each fresh ordinary launch, atomic ordinary-group member and fresh GUI/MIDI Piano SET_RETRIGGER Native birth SHALL supply zero PCM in every channel before its declared physical birth origin. It SHALL NOT wrap a Normal loop tail, translate predecessor source PCM or borrow already processed predecessor content as that new birth's pre-origin input. The silence SHALL be explicit birth-context provenance, never a claim of actually read or previously heard source history. This is a narrow exception to the earlier-source-origin proof in prepare-key-lock-realtime-state's Source History Has Explicit Forward Provenance; genuine existing-source SET, resume, replay and history-mode preparation SHALL retain that proof and their original behavior.

The new birth's selected physical beginning and logical source endpoint SHALL remain fixed until actual emission at E, with original T unchanged. Positive-time input lookahead MAY use only the existing held prepared-feed/Native/FIFO ownership with separate actual-input coordinates; it SHALL NOT authorize unheard E minus T source/filter consumption or advance the logical birth. Native history H, raw discard and adapter lead SHALL remain bounded explicit coordinates under the existing preparation path. This SHALL NOT add a pipeline or delay, change Native profile, markers or BPM, or select a musical compensation constant.

A supported changing-rate EndpointReset birth SHALL use silent pre-origin PCM while retaining all genuine journal and frozen-future evidence needed for its exact accepted E rate state. It SHALL still prove contiguous history coverage, the actual-input future seam, reader/source/selection contracts, current permit and own voice generation, historical pitch/reset sequence and the bounded monotonic H fixed point over the relevant pitch profiles. Zero PCM SHALL NOT replace that evidence, rewrite the actual journal or authorize unsupported past/paused/young/missing/gapped/stale/cancelled histories. The actual E ratio, target and remaining active-frame smoothing interval SHALL remain independent of physical phase reset.

Eligible cache/reserve hits and worker misses SHALL use the same exact birth context and source/render authority. Each attack's mutable Native/FIFO owner SHALL remain independent. Measurement geometry and receipts SHALL describe the same silent prefix, physical origin, admitted rate/pitch/reset sequence and block phase, or report unavailable evidence. A receipt from wrapped or shifted pre-origin PCM SHALL NOT authorize a silent birth or confer accepted musical C. Common group E, all-member validation before first claim, cancellation, source/selection/ABA/ACK lifetimes, last-reader retirement and realtime boundaries SHALL remain unchanged. Complete physical context SHALL NOT establish acoustic alignment, distinct start-policy emission, click safety, device or Human acceptance.

#### Scenario: A remote unplayed loop tail cannot change a fresh beginning
- **GIVEN** independently constructed A/B sources have the same selected physical attack and all positive-time source content required for a bounded emitted capture
- **AND** their only difference is a remote loop tail unreachable by that capture's legitimate forward input
- **WHEN** real public ordinary or Piano SET_RETRIGGER births emit their complete beginnings through the existing Native path
- **THEN** both complete emitted beginnings SHALL match an independently initialized silence-then-source Native reference without slicing either emitted output
- **AND** the new Native input before the physical origin SHALL contain no wrapped or shifted remote-tail PCM

#### Scenario: Cache and worker paths retain identical birth context
- **GIVEN** equivalent fresh originals use an eligible on-time cache/reserve hit and an existing bounded worker preparation miss
- **WHEN** each emits from its truthful actual E under the same admitted source and rate state
- **THEN** their complete beginnings SHALL use identical silent pre-origin context and the selected physical origin, with exact source/render and action provenance
- **AND** a cache with a different context SHALL use the existing bounded reprepare/refusal path without adopting that incompatible owner
- **AND** an ordinary group SHALL retain common E and complete all-member validation before its first effect claim

#### Scenario: Active glide proof survives silent birth input
- **GIVEN** a supported active-glide EndpointReset request has genuine journal, reader, future-seam, own-generation and historical pitch evidence reaching exact E
- **WHEN** its new Native owner is prepared with silent pre-origin PCM
- **THEN** the same coverage, H fixed-point and historical pitch/reset checks SHALL remain mandatory
- **AND** the physical beginning SHALL retain exact accepted E ratio, target and smoothing-step remainder under unequal callback partitions
- **AND** the predecessor's journal and audio SHALL remain unchanged before the original complete effect claim

#### Scenario: Silent context does not create missing chronology
- **GIVEN** an EndpointReset request has an unsupported past or paused glide, a young/missing/gapped journal, a stale future seam or a failed reader, source, selection, generation, ABA, ACK or cancellation guard
- **WHEN** its new birth declares silent pre-origin PCM
- **THEN** the whole action SHALL remain refused without changing old/peer audio, original T, parameters or effective feedback
- **AND** zero PCM, waiting or a matching final endpoint SHALL NOT repair that missing authority

#### Scenario: Attack measurement must describe the actual birth context
- **GIVEN** a fresh birth requires an attack-correspondence proposal
- **WHEN** its geometry or receipt was obtained from wrapped/shifted pre-origin PCM or a different physical source, rate/pitch/reset sequence or block phase
- **THEN** the evidence SHALL remain unavailable for that birth unless recomputed and validated for its exact silent context
- **AND** a compatible proposal SHALL still leave musical C, Native acoustic alignment and listening acceptance open

### Requirement: Fresh phase reset preserves the independently accepted rate clock
The system SHALL preserve the actual accepted source-rate state evaluated at a supported fresh retrigger's actual E, including actual ratio, rate target and remaining active-frame interval before the next smoothing step. Resetting physical phase to the selected beginning MUST NOT reset that rate clock, substitute its final target ratio or create a note-to-tempo coupling. Required preparation SHALL use genuine captured history or a supported certified forward trajectory on the existing worker. An unproved past-T glide, paused pending glide or unsupported source/selection history SHALL remain an explicit whole-action refusal; full-onset semantics SHALL NOT create missing history or relax original source/control/ABA/Stop/cancellation guards. Native prehistory and adapter lookahead SHALL remain separate from emitted new-source progress.

#### Scenario: A supported glide reaches a fresh retrigger effect
- **GIVEN** the original action's supported rate/control evidence determines actual ratio, target and smoothing-step remainder at E
- **WHEN** its full physical beginning is adopted atomically at E
- **THEN** the beginning SHALL use that exact rate-step state and advance the existing accepted rate trajectory through actually active emitted frames
- **AND** physical phase reset SHALL NOT restart smoothing, retime T, change BPM or borrow the predecessor's Native/filter state

#### Scenario: A phase reset cannot authorize an unavailable glide
- **GIVEN** an original request has no supported past-T or paused-glide rate/control proof or has a stale source, ACK, selection or ABA fence
- **WHEN** it requests a complete new beginning
- **THEN** the whole request SHALL remain refused without changing the old audio, parameters, original T or effective feedback
- **AND** waiting, a matching endpoint or a fresh phase reset SHALL NOT supply the missing authority

#### Scenario: Resume and nonstarting SET keep genuine existing-source chronology
- **GIVEN** an existing voice owns actual source phase, rate-step state, Native/FIFO and filter history
- **WHEN** it resumes or accepts a non-retriggering SET under its existing contract
- **THEN** the full-birth rule SHALL NOT restart that source or filter history
- **AND** pause SHALL retain its existing active-frame freeze and actual replay SHALL retain its original source-feed and voice-lifetime fences

### Requirement: Full physical births preserve admission and atomic authority
Complete-onset preparation SHALL retain each ordinary or fresh Piano original's content lifetime, sequence, timestamp, source/control revision, own ACK, selected render tuple, cancellation and bounded work authority. Each new mutable voice/Native/filter owner SHALL remain independent even when immutable PCM is shared. Rate or loop ABA, stale source, cancellation, reached resource bounds and unavailable required material SHALL remain explicit whole-action failures. Every ordinary group member SHALL use a common actual E with all-member validation before the first effect claim. Native construction and heavy preparation or retirement SHALL remain off realtime; the callback SHALL use the existing scheduler and bounded owner exchange. Complete physical onset SHALL NOT certify Native acoustic alignment, distinct first-sound/attack-policy emission, musical quality, device accuracy or Human acceptance.

#### Scenario: Native worker misses its planned execution endpoint
- **GIVEN** an ordinary or fresh Piano original owns a fully selected physical source beginning, supported rate clock and exact render tuple
- **WHEN** its preparation returns after planned E within existing bounded attempts
- **THEN** the same original may obtain a later truthful actual E without discarding its un-emitted source prefix
- **AND** the existing source, control, lifetime and atomic effect guards remain mandatory
