# Preserve the complete first attack of a new launch

## Why
An original GUI PAD or Piano retrigger captures requested T before its effect.
The former preparation advanced its source by E-T before any PCM was emitted. Even
neutral dry playback can consequently omit the entire unique first kick. The
Human's 2026-10-20 complete-onset instruction supersedes that suffix recipe.

The remaining Native context gap is separate: a fresh Normal endpoint currently
can derive pre-origin input by wrapping to the loop tail, and EndpointReset can
translate old source spans into a virtual pre-birth source history. A new physical
attack has no already-played loop tail. Reconcile that fresh-birth context with
the existing explicit forward-history contract before changing preparation;
genuine SET, resume and replay still require their original history proof.

## What changes
Separate requested target T, actual effect E, physical source birth and the
accepted rate-step clock. New ordinary and fresh GUI/MIDI SET_RETRIGGER launches
emit the selected physical beginning at E without consuming an unheard prefix.
Retain the actual accepted ratio, target and smoothing-step remainder evaluated
at E independently from phase reset, truthful lateness and original scalar
source/control/ACK/ABA/cancellation/claim guards. Existing-source SET, resume and
actual replay retain their chronology; unproved past/paused glides stay refused.
Use the same group, worker, scheduler, source reader, Native owner and PAD DSP.
Diagnose Native start response separately and complete both real start policies.

Declare physically silent Native input before each fresh ordinary/group or Piano
SET_RETRIGGER birth, with provenance distinct from actually heard source history.
Reuse existing bounded worker, adapter, H accounting and silent-input preparation.
Preserve exact E rate-clock proof for active glides, all journal/future-seam/reader/
own-generation and historical pitch checks, and the complete physical source
beginning. Cache/reserve hits, worker misses and any attack measurement must share
that same birth context. Prove independence from unreachable remote loop tails;
do not infer musical compensation or accepted attack timing from that proof.

## Non-goals
No fixed compensation offsets, marker edits, gain boost, new scheduler, alternate
player, altered master clock, or fabricated musical/device/Human acceptance.
Complete physical onset alone does not establish Native acoustic alignment or
distinct musical first-sound/attack-policy emission.

## Realtime

Existing worker preparation, bounded retries and atomic adoption remain.
