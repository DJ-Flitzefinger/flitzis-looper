## Why

The delivered Source Map editor uses the canonical Native B/S evaluator, while
the existing Rubber Band comparison still supplies two collinear keyframes.
Its affine mechanism evidence cannot establish compatibility with an accepted
nonuniform map. A measured comparison is required before mapped live adoption.

## What Changes

- Bind an isolated, test-only comparison to complete synthetic PCM and an actual
  validated SourceBeatmap, preserving stable source/map identities.
- Derive canonical source positions and positive Stretcher keyframes from that
  same evaluator, with an independent predeclared coordinate oracle.
- Reuse the existing fractional source reader, Native/FIFO adapter and offline
  Stretcher owner; measure complete audio, pitch and timing separately.
- Retain every failed original acoustic predicate and explicit coverage refusal.

## Non-goals

No production SYNC switch, callback map adoption, new renderer, model/default
cutover, fitted delay/gain/crop, or device/listening acceptance. The full B5 joint
domain and later B6/B7/B8/P6/Human gates remain required.

## Realtime

All map construction, evaluation, allocation, processing and reporting belongs
to the isolated non-realtime diagnostic. Production callback paths are unchanged.
